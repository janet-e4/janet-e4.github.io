05 Hard questions

Ask it The questions hype usually dodges

You should be skeptical. Here are the direct answers.

01 Will this actually work for my business?

No one can honestly guarantee that before seeing the business. The difference is the process: Core Cell is built around your real work over 90 days, then tested against that work before handover. The first conversation is where we decide whether that is a credible fit.

02 What happens when my business changes?

The memory is meant to be maintained, not frozen. Procedures, preferences, and exceptions can be updated as the company changes. The 90-day work includes learning how those updates happen.

03 What if CREATAIV disappears?

The installed Core Cell system and its business memory remain on the machine you control. The system is yours. Any external model or backup service you choose remains subject to that provider’s account and terms.

04 What about security and privacy?

Core Cell’s business memory lives on your machine. You choose the AI model and backup connected to it, and those providers have their own data terms. Some additional privacy and safety controls are still on the roadmap; ask about any requirement that is non-negotiable for you.

05 Am I going to feel dumb using this?

The main interface is conversation, not code. There is still a learning curve because the system is being fitted to a real business, but the handover is part of the work—not an instruction link sent after purchase.

06 How is this different from what I tried before?

The tool you tried was probably a general-purpose destination. Core Cell starts with the business memory, places it on your machine, connects the intelligence you choose, and spends 90 days fitting the system to real work.
