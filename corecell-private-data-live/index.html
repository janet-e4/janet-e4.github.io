What CREATAIV is building

A careful redaction direction.

The intended workflow is designed around a simple principle: a model should receive only the material it needs for the task, not the private details it does not.

IN ACTIVE DEVELOPMENTThis is the direction—not a finished feature, security claim, or confidentiality guarantee.

01

Mask private details

The workflow would identify and strip names, account details, and other sensitive elements before model processing.

02

Process the masked version

The selected model would work from the reduced, masked document rather than the original client file.

03

Restore approved details

After processing, the local workflow would reinsert the approved private details into the result under owner control.

Plain statement: you cannot buy this redaction workflow as a completed feature today. CREATAIV is sharing the intended direction early so a confidentiality-bound professional can evaluate the idea without being sold a promise that has not shipped.

Ask the questions that matter

The hard questions are welcome.

A careful buyer should press on the boundaries. These are the answers we can stand behind today.

Will my clients' data ever leave my hands or train an AI?

Core Cell stores your working files on your machine, and CREATAIV does not take custody of them. You choose the model and decide what, if anything, a configured workflow sends to it. If you choose a hosted model, that provider's terms, retention practices, and your professional duties still govern the material you submit. BYO-LLM gives you choice; it does not erase those terms.

Can I actually use AI on privileged documents?

That is the use case the redaction workflow is being built to address, but it is not ready today. The direction is to mask private details, process a reduced working copy, and restore approved details locally afterward. Until a reviewed workflow ships, your professional duties and the selected model's terms still control what is appropriate to submit.

Are you promising encryption or a confidentiality guarantee?

No. Owner-only encryption and related safeguards are roadmap directions, not live features claimed on this site. We will not promise a confidentiality guarantee until the product and legal review support that promise.

What happens to my files if CREATAIV disappears?

Your Core Cell, working files, per-version license, and owner-controlled backup remain with the machine and destinations you control. Our business disappearing would not take those things with it.

Who is accountable for the legal side of these claims?

CREATAIV routes public privacy and compliance claims through legal review—including with Bay Law—before publishing them. That process limits what we say; it does not replace your own professional judgment, client duties, or matter-specific legal analysis.
