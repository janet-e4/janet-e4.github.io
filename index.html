Ask the questions that matter

The hard questions are welcome.

You have run real things. You know the value of asking what happens when plans change.

Who can actually see my stuff?

Your working data lives on your machine, in your name. We are building toward stronger owner-only access at the technical level; encryption at rest is a direction, not a shipped promise today.

What happens if CREATAIV goes out of business?

Your Core Cell lives on your machine, in your name. We disappear; your machine and your data do not.

Am I renting this like everything else?

No. You buy Core Cell and own the license per version. You can leave with your machine, your working files, and the backup you control.

What if someone steals my computer or comes after my data?

That risk deserves a serious answer. CREATAIV is planning additional safeguards, including a kill-switch direction for future versions. It is not a live feature today, and we will not pretend otherwise.

Do I have to be technical?

No. If you can hold a conversation, you can run this. You talk; Janet works. Fritz is there when something needs sorting out.

Which AI company am I stuck with?

None. You pick the intelligence and can swap it whenever you want. That independence is part of the point.
