One system 02

The idea

Departments become smart knowledge lanes—not new silos.

Departments are more than org boxes. They are the places knowledge is stored, maintained, and retrieved. Core Cell makes those lanes explicit: Sales, Service, Operations, and Marketing can organize the company’s methods, history, and decisions so the right role can draw on the right context.

The same owner-controlled Core Cell keeps those lanes connected, so the business can answer with one operating voice instead of four competing versions.

What this means: you choose what to teach Core Cell and how the lanes are organized. It does not claim to ingest every app automatically, and useful results depend on the knowledge your team maintains.

How it works

You talk. It organizes. Your team retrieves by role.

Core Cell is built around the way a small company already teaches: through conversation, examples, files, decisions, and the language people use every day. You choose the AI behind it, can swap that model, and control where its backup lives.

01 / TALK

Explain the work, provide the source material you choose, and correct it as the business changes. There is no separate language to learn.

03 / RETRIEVE

Ask directly 05

Hard questions you can ask

No mystery box. Ask what changes for your team.

01How painful is the rollout across my team?
People work with Core Cell through ordinary conversation rather than another dense dashboard. The 90-day Move In → Hand It Over → Run It program gives the rollout a guided order, starting with the highest-value lane. It is designed to reduce disruption, not to promise a zero-effort deployment.
02Will our knowledge finally stop being siloed?
That is the operating goal: departments become connected smart lanes that people can retrieve from by role. Core Cell supplies the shared structure; the quality of the result still depends on what your team teaches, corrects, and maintains.
03Will it replace the software we already run?
No. Core Cell sits alongside your existing line-of-business tools. It gives the company an owned knowledge and retrieval layer without asking you to rip out the software that already runs sales, service, operations, or marketing.
04How do per-seat costs scale as we grow?
You own the licensed system, and the recommended Hive Mind business tier is a flat $175 per month with your own Buzz install rather than a charge multiplied by every seat. Required day-91 support is a separate $650 per month, billed quarterly.
05Whose AI and whose data?
You choose the model behind Core Cell and can change it. Your business knowledge remains on your machine, you control the backup destination, and the licensed Core Cell version is yours rather than a dependency on one model vendor.
