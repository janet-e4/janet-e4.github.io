# CreatAIv Brand Style Guide

## Introduction

This document outlines the visual identity system for **CreatAIv**. The core of the identity is the "Abstract Hive" concept—a stylized hexagonal "C" incorporating a subtle honeycomb pattern, representing AI worker bees driving business results. This system has been specifically designed to scale down beautifully for product sites while maintaining high visual impact in large formats.

## 1. Logo System & Layouts

The CreatAIv logo system is designed for flexibility across different applications.

| Variation | Primary Use Case | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Horizontal Layout** | Website headers, letterheads, standard applications | The primary logo. Icon on the left, wordmark on the right. Best for wide spaces. |
| **Stacked Layout** | T-shirts, social media profiles, square formats | Icon centered above the wordmark. Best when vertical space is available. |
| **Icon Only (Brandmark)** | Favicons, product "Powered By" badges, app icons | The standalone hexagonal "C" mark. Designed to be recognizable even at 16x16 pixels. |

## 2. Color Palette

The brand utilizes a sci-fi/tech-forward palette anchored in dark space, illuminated by teal and gold.

### Primary Brand Colors
- **CreatAIv Teal:** `#008687` (Primary color for the icon outer ring)
- **CreatAIv Cyan:** `#00A8A9` (Used for brighter highlights in the icon)
- **CreatAIv Gold:** `#D6A54D` (Accent color used on icon vertices and the "AI" in the wordmark)

### Background Colors
- **Dark Background (Primary):** `#010204` (Near-black, the primary canvas for the brand)
- **Light Background (Secondary):** `#FFFFFF` (Clean white, used when dark mode is not available)
- **Charcoal Text:** `#0A0F1A` (Used for the wordmark when placed on a white background)

## 3. Color Variations & Merchandising

To ensure the logo works across all physical and digital mediums, specific colorways have been established.

| Background/Material | Approved Logo Variant |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Dark Web / Black T-Shirts** | Full Color (Teal + Gold) OR All-White Monochrome |
| **Light Web / White T-Shirts** | Full Color (Teal + Gold + Charcoal Text) OR All-Black Monochrome |
| **Gray T-Shirts / Mid-tones** | All-White Monochrome |

*Note: When using the monochrome versions (all-white or all-black), the entire logo including the "AI" text must be in the single solid color. Do not mix monochrome with the gold accent.*

## 4. Typography

The typographic system reinforces the futuristic, technological nature of the company.

- **Logo Wordmark:** Custom geometric sans-serif (similar to Orbitron).
- **Primary Headings:** Orbitron, sans-serif. Used for all major headlines on the website to maintain the tech aesthetic.
- **Body Copy:** Rajdhani, sans-serif. Used for paragraphs and UI elements for high legibility while maintaining a squared-off, modern feel.

## 5. Usage Rules & Clear Space

To maintain the integrity of the brand, the logo must be given room to breathe.

1. **Clear Space:** Always maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "C" in the wordmark. No other elements or text should enter this zone.
2. **Minimum Size:** The wordmark versions should never be reproduced smaller than 1 inch (25mm) wide in print, or 100 pixels wide on screen.
3. **Favicon Usage:** For sizes smaller than 100 pixels (like the upper-left corner of product sites or browser tabs), the **Icon Only** variant must be used.
4. **Proportions:** Never stretch, skew, or distort the logo. Always scale proportionally.
5. **Contrast:** Ensure high contrast against backgrounds. Do not place the full-color logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a darkening overlay.

## 6. The "Powered By" Product Application

When CreatAIv acts as the parent company for a product (e.g., Donna Sue), the **Icon Only** brandmark should be used as a "maker's mark." It should be placed unobtrusively (typically in the footer or a secondary header position) to brand the product without overpowering the product's own identity.
