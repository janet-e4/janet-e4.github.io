06 / Hard questions

Ask the question you are actually thinking.

Small teams do not have time for a glossy answer that creates three more problems.

Will this disrupt the tools we already run?

No. Your POS and existing software stay where they are. Core Cell sits alongside them. Any connection to another system is reviewed for fit rather than sold as a magical universal integration.

Will my staff actually use it?

They talk to it like a person; there is no new all-purpose dashboard to master. If they can hold a conversation, they can use it. Adoption still depends on teaching it useful work and making it part of the team’s normal rhythm.

Is this cheaper than hiring?

It is designed to add coverage and multiplication without a new payroll line. Whether it is cheaper than a particular hire depends on the role, your workload, and the value of the capacity you build. The full Core Cell cost is shown above so you can compare honestly.

Do I own it, or am I renting?

You own the licensed version: the data stays on your machine, the backup destination stays in your name, and you choose the AI behind it. You can leave with your data and your setup rather than starting over empty-handed.
